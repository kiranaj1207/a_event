About The Competition

Where Analytical Instinct Turns Into Strategic Impact

IMARTICUS DATATHON 2026 is an intense, high-energy college data analytics competition designed by industry educators at Imarticus Learning. Teams analyse datasets, identify business problems, and present data-driven solutions to industry judges.

Open to ambitious students across disciplines:
BCA BBA B.Com B.E / B.Tech B.Sc MBA MCA + All Relevant Streams
Competition Structure

The 4-Stage Arena Journey

Progress through 4 meticulously crafted stages simulating the end-to-end workflow of a top-tier management consultant and data analyst.

Stage 01 • 30 Mins

Round 1 – Data Quiz

Tests logical reasoning, data interpretation and analytical thinking. Rapid-fire scenario questions designed to evaluate how quickly your team identifies patterns under time pressure.

  • Logical deduction and numerical reasoning puzzles
  • Chart & table interpretation under sprint clock
Stage 02 • 60 Mins

Round 2 – Data Detective

Analyse real-world business scenarios and identify meaningful insights from raw datasets. Uncover anomalies, spot hidden revenue leaks, and determine why customer behavior shifted.

  • Raw multi-tab corporate case files provided on-site
  • Identify root causes and formulate strategic hypotheses
Stage 03 • 75 Mins

Round 3 – Data Visualization Challenge

Create dashboards, charts and visual reports using Excel or Google Sheets. Convert complex calculations into executive-ready dashboards that speak directly to decision-makers.

Executive Pulse Dashboard

Conversion +34.8% ▲ 4.2% vs target
Data Accuracy 99.4% ● Verified
ROI Index 3.8x ▲ Scaled
  • KPI cards, dynamic charts, heatmaps & slicers
  • Evaluated on clarity, visual hierarchy and aesthetic power
Stage 04 • 45 Mins

Round 4 – Business Case Presentation

Present solutions and recommendations to a panel of judges. Step onto the stage with your team and pitch actionable business strategies backed by indisputable data evidence.

  • 5-minute slide deck presentation to jury panel
  • Live Q&A defending analytical assumptions and ROI models
Grand Finale • The Podium

Finale – Awards & Recognition

Recognize outstanding teams and celebrate their achievements. Outstanding performers take home championship trophies, merit awards, and guaranteed corporate visibility.

🏆 Winner & Runner-up Trophies 📜 Certificate of Excellence 🚀 Imarticus Career Sponsorship
